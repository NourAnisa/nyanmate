# NyanMate Mascot Guide

NyanMate's default mascot is an original gray-and-white chibi kitten designed to be recognizable at small desktop-overlay sizes.

## Signature look

- Soft gray top/head patches and back patch
- White face, chest, paws, and belly
- Large warm dark eyes with bright highlights
- Small pink nose and blush cheeks
- Burgundy scarf as the permanent signature accessory
- Small gold star badge on the scarf
- Rounded fluffy proportions with a large head and compact body

## Activity modes

- **Default / Idle**: scarf only, gentle floating and tail movement
- **Thinking**: thought bubble and head tilt
- **Coding**: blue headphones and compact laptop
- **Teaching**: glasses, graduation cap, and pointer
- **Success**: sparkles and stronger blush
- **Error**: alert marker and shake animation
- **Sleeping**: closed eyes, slow tail, floating Zs

## Animation principles

Animations should remain subtle so NyanMate does not distract from work or cover presentation content. Prefer low-cost CSS/sprite animation and reduce frame activity while idle.

The current CSS mascot is a lightweight runtime representation. Future pixel-art sprite sheets should preserve this visual identity rather than copying any third-party desktop-pet artwork.
